## Assignment Overview

Section Element: C03 Info Card
<!-- Try also: V01 Left-Border Accent Box, T06 Styled Blockquote, or plain paragraphs under T01. -->

Research art, design, and technical projects that have happened within the last 12 months. Find examples of things you thought were impossible that people are now making. Emphasize work that is open-source, well documented, forkable, remixable, or otherwise shareable.

Your post should describe what each project does, why it surprised you, and what design question it opens for your own work in this course.

## Requirements

## Rubric

Rubric Scale Tag: 4-point
<!-- Try also: percentage-first, compact, expanded-feedback, or criterion-cards. -->

### Research Quality

Weight: 30%
4: Examples are current, relevant, and well sourced
3: Examples are relevant with adequate sourcing
2: Examples are loosely connected or missing important source context
1: Examples are unclear, outdated, or unsupported

### Analysis and Surprise

Weight: 30%
4: Clearly explains why each example seemed newly possible or surprising
3: Explains most examples with some thoughtful analysis
2: Descriptions stay mostly at the surface
1: Little analysis beyond naming the examples

### Connection to Course Questions

Weight: 25%
4: Strongly connects examples to agents, phone interactions, openness, or AI-assisted design practice
3: Makes a clear course connection
2: Course connection is present but thin
1: Course connection is missing or unclear

### Presentation and Documentation

Weight: 15%
4: Clear writing, useful links, image captions, and accessible image descriptions
3: Complete and readable with minor issues
2: Missing some links, captions, or accessible descriptions
1: Difficult to follow or missing key submission pieces
